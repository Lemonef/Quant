## Data ceiling

The unchanged protocol has a 200-bar feature warm-up and requires at least 250 valid training bars. With four walk-forward folds, the latest available training cutoff is three-quarters through the sample, so at least 600 resampled bars are needed even before the confirmation purge. The timeframes marked SKIPPED therefore could not be tested with available history. This caps how far the higher-timeframe idea can be pushed.
